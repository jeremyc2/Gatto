# Rooftops kitten coin

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool using the user's gold kitten
coin as a visual reference. The transparent master is `kitten-coin.png`.
Rooftops prefilters it to a small texture once when the game is created.

## Generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: single collectible gold coin sprite for a small platform game. Input image is reference only for the cute baby kitten and gold relief style. Create a new perfectly round coin viewed directly face-on, isolated on a truly transparent background, square composition with coin filling 94% of frame and tiny clear margins. A very cute baby kitten with large round shiny eyes, pointed ears, rounded cheeks, tiny nose and clear whiskers, sitting with two little front paws, sculpted as bright raised gold bas-relief on a dark bronze-gold recessed field. Enlarge kitten head so it occupies most of coin face and remains unmistakably a baby CAT when displayed at 28 pixels. Body and paws small at bottom. Fine fur engraving, thick luxurious concentric polished golden rims, milled ornamental edging and subtle floral engraved flourishes low at sides similar to reference but simplified. High contrast luminous light gold kitten against dark recessed field. Soft top-left metallic highlights, no cast shadow outside coin. No text, no words, no letters, no numbers, no watermark anywhere. Avoid realistic photograph scenery, tilted perspective, checkerboard, white background. The coin must be completely contained in frame, crisp circle and transparent exterior.
