# Gatto walkthrough

[Overview](../README.md) · [Setup](SETUP.md) · [Support](../SUPPORT.md)

Use Gatto to upload screenshots and videos, then paste them into GitHub
conversations. If you haven't connected GitHub yet, start with [Setup](SETUP.md).

Hover over icons for short hints, and explore the **⋯** menus as you go.

## Your first upload

1. **Choose a repository.** Open Gatto and select the GitHub repository you're
   working with.
2. **Add your images or videos.** Paste an image with **Command+V**, drag files
   into Gatto, or click the drop area to choose them. You can add several at once.

   <img src="images/walkthrough/framed/03-image-staged.png" alt="A screenshot ready to upload" width="800">

   <img src="images/walkthrough/framed/06-video-staged.png" alt="A video ready to upload" width="800">

3. **Upload, copy, and paste.** Click **Upload**. When an attachment says
   **Uploaded**, use its **Copy** button and paste into your issue, pull request,
   comment, or discussion.

<img src="images/walkthrough/framed/04-upload-complete.png" alt="An uploaded screenshot ready to copy" width="800">

For fewer steps, use the arrow beside the upload button to choose **Upload and
copy**. Choosing it uploads and copies your attachments, and Gatto remembers the
choice for next time.

Uploading sends every attachment that still needs uploading, even if only some
are selected. Use **Copy all uploaded** when you want to share the completed
attachments together.

You can also give attachments useful titles and descriptions before sharing.

<img src="images/walkthrough/framed/11-attachment-details.png" alt="Editing an attachment's title and description" width="800">

## Keep your work organized

**Scratchpad** is a handy place for quick, everyday work. Use **Create Project**
when you'd like to keep related attachments together. Each project remembers
its own repository.

<img src="images/walkthrough/framed/10-project.png" alt="A project and its attachments beside the project sidebar" width="800">

Your work is saved as you go, so you can switch projects or close the window
and return later. Attachments you've already uploaded are ready to copy again.
Pin projects you use often to keep them easy to find.

<img src="images/walkthrough/framed/12-attachment-reorder.png" alt="Selected attachments and the actions available for them" width="800">

## Mark up an image

Click an image to open the editor. Add an arrow, write a note, highlight a detail,
or use numbered markers to explain a sequence. You can also pixelate an area.
Try the tools and use **Undo** whenever you want to revisit a change.

<img src="images/walkthrough/framed/image-editor.png" alt="A screenshot with a note and an arrow added in the editor" width="820">

<img src="images/walkthrough/framed/image-editor-text-editing.png" alt="Writing a note directly on a screenshot" width="820">

<img src="images/walkthrough/framed/image-editor-shapes.png" alt="Shapes and arrows to try in the editor" width="820">

<img src="images/walkthrough/framed/image-editor-markers.png" alt="Numbered steps with descriptions" width="820">

Click **Save** when you're happy with the result. Your original file stays
unchanged. If you edit an image that was already uploaded, upload it again
before copying the new version.

## Compare two images

Select two images, then choose **Combine into side-by-side comparison** above
the attachment list. Gatto keeps the originals and adds a comparison you can
share as one image.

Open the comparison to rename its captions or add notes, then save, upload,
and copy it like any other image.

<img src="images/walkthrough/framed/comparison-editor.png" alt="Two images together in a comparison with editable captions" width="820">

## Add from Clipboard

Copy an image or a video file, then choose **Add from Clipboard** from Gatto's menu bar
menu. By default, it saves your item in the background and shows a notification.
Choose **Upload and copy** in that notification when you're ready to share it.

This uses Scratchpad by default. To use another project, open that project's
**⋯** menu and choose **Use for "Add from Clipboard"**.

For a one-step upload and copy, turn on **Automatically copy "Add from clipboard"**
in App Preferences. You can also enable a global shortcut there to use Add from
Clipboard while you're working in another app.

## Uploads and retries

If an upload fails, try **Upload** again. Gatto retries the unfinished attachments
and keeps the ones that succeeded.

<img src="images/walkthrough/framed/07-mixed-upload-progress.png" alt="A batch of attachments at different stages of uploading" width="800">

For a larger video, Gatto may offer **Compress**. This makes a smaller copy and
leaves your original file unchanged. Preview the result before sharing it.

<img src="images/walkthrough/framed/08-video-too-large.png" alt="Gatto offers to compress a larger video" width="800">

<img src="images/walkthrough/framed/09-video-compressing.png" alt="A video being compressed before upload" width="800">

Gatto stays in the menu bar when you close its window. You can return from there
whenever you're ready; let active uploads finish before quitting the app.
