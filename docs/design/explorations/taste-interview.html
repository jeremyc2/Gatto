A visual taste interview

A small, unscored interview with side-by-side evidence. Make the starting preferences first; then pressure-test the decisions that depend on them. Copy everything as Markdown with one button.

Your draft stays in this browser when storage is available.

This is a conversation in two rounds, not a scored test. Nothing is preselected. Choose “unsure” or “none” when that is the honest answer. Recommendations are tucked away so you can form your own reaction first.

Round one · the decisions we can ask now

These are independent starting preferences. The next round is built only after you respond; it will not silently decide unresolved questions for you.

Round two · pressure-test those preferences

These questions depend on your first-round answers. If an earlier choice changes, this round resets so stale answers are not treated as decisions.

Your reactions

Send the useful part back

Your choices, reasons, confidence, and current visual settings become Markdown. No account, submission, or automatic sharing.

Preview or manually copy the Markdown
