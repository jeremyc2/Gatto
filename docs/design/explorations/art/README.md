## Prompt set used

The prompts below preserve the art-direction briefs and generation inputs. Generated results are inspected as outputs, not assumed to obey every requested construction detail exactly.

### Watercolor favorite

An exquisite traditional watercolor and reed-pen ink character-development sheet on warm ivory paper: a lovable detailed black kitten with big luminous cyan eyes, soft round cheeks, small muzzle, lavender inner ears, four small paws, and one thick expressive tail. Six separate full-body views, three columns by two rows, generous margins and consistent character design. Top row: curious seated with one paw up; carrying a small image card; delighted waving. Bottom row: curled resting; thoughtful head tilt; small playful hop. Handcrafted translucent pigment blooms, charcoal-black ink contour, expressive fine whiskers, paper showing through highlights, delicately modeled fur washes and thoughtful anatomy. Sophisticated illustrated-book painting, light ivory ground, no blue glow or smooth digital cel shading. No costuming, rocket packs, text, labels, borders, or watermarks. High-resolution landscape output. Generated without image references to preserve a materially different treatment.

### Paper favorite

A physical paper-sculpture character-development sheet photographed on a pale warm-gray ground. An adorable charcoal-black kitten built from intricately cut and curved textured paper, soft muzzle, round cheeks, oversized cyan-blue inset eyes, lilac inner ears, four small layered paws, and a thick curled tail. Visible fibers, cut edges, layered dimensional shadows, neutral photographic light, no neon glow. Six separate full-body poses in the same three-by-two arrangement: alert raised paw, carrying a pale card, happy waving, curled resting, puzzled tilt, little hop. Refined tactile craft and expressive anatomy, not geometric clipart. No clothing, rocket pack, text, labels, borders, or watermarks. Generated without image references.

### Luminous companion

Use Gatto's existing rocket-cat illustration only as identity guidance. Reimagine a cuter, more developed older kitten with rounded cheeks, a compact body, expressive cyan-blue eyes, lavender inner ears, natural feline anatomy and four small paws. Six separate poses in equal three-by-two cells: curious, carrying an image card, delighted, resting, thoughtful, hopping. Refined hand-painted animated-character rendering, velvety dark fur, delicate cheek and ear accents, cool reflected light and expressive eyes. No text, logos, costume, or rocket pack on the sheet. A background-removal edit was requested while preserving the character and placement; the saved output still retains visible atmospheric background and should be treated accordingly. The extra accepted variants explore more textured and layered fur within this luminous family.

### Shared instruction for the composed postcards

Create an original, richly detailed mixed-media storybook illustration, landscape three-by-two, using the watercolor favorite as the main reference, layered paper second, and Gatto's existing rocket-cat art only for a complementary smooth animated character. Different materials must coexist in one scene, with individual personality, proportions, eye color, height, ears, muzzle, and tail. Use a shared ground, believable scale, soft illumination, contact shadows, and related attention. A small pale picture of a violet pansy connects the action. Keep pigment as pigment and cut paper as cut paper. Use a simple warm ivory/celadon place with breathing room. No dogs, humans, comic panels, speech balloons, halftones, RGB offsets, portals, superhero imagery, text, or watermarks. No repeated contact-sheet poses. Original characters only.

The input images were [cat-watercolor.png](cat-watercolor.png), [cat-paper.png](cat-paper.png), and the existing [rocket cat](../../../../packaging/rocket-cat-transparent.png).

**Handoff scene:** two cats on a low workbench. A smaller watercolor-and-ink black kitten with teal eyes leans forward to offer one corner of the pansy picture. A slightly taller charcoal-paper kitten with amber eyes reaches to accept the other. Their paws share the object and their gazes connect. Different tail rhythms, a tiny paint jar and curled paper offcut, the beginning of a friendship and a useful handoff.

**Discovery scene:** a watercolor black kitten with teal eyes lies belly-down on a pale ledge, head and paw lowered to inspect a picture below. A smaller app-like midnight kitten with violet-blue eyes steadies the picture from another position; a small cobalt rocket pack is switched off. Different heights, ears, tails, and postures; common soft light and shadow. The output mixes the visitor's paper-like texture with app-associated details, so it is a directional composition rather than proof of a finalized smooth-cel design.

**Together scene:** three distinct materials around the completed pansy picture. A watercolor black cat sits in a three-quarter back view looking toward friends. A larger amber-eyed paper kitten reclines on a little stack of cream sheets. A smaller smooth midnight kitten with violet-blue eyes and a switched-off cobalt pack leans its paws over the ledge. Unique actions and proportions, a simple plant and warm window light, the small useful thing finished.

## Composition limits still worth discussing

The generated worlds are more detailed and botanical than the earliest low-fidelity scene brief. That is material to judge, not a requirement for the eventual site. In particular, the pansy painting is an illustrative shared object, not a proposed logo or a promise that Gatto transforms image contents. A secondary cat's exact identity and coat color remain open. The main app character remains the black-cat anchor.
